Nancy Zhang

Ge Li and Ning Zhao Professor of Statistics
Department of Statistics and Data Science, The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania
Member, Graduate Group in Genomics and Computational Biology

CV· Google Scholar· Wharton profile· Email

I came to statistics through biology. While I was finishing a mathematics degree at Stanford, the first draft of the human genome was nearing completion, microarrays were beginning to change how biological research was done, and the term “GWAS” had not yet been coined. The idea that quantitative models, applied to data from high-throughput experiments, could give a systemic understanding of biology was hard to resist.

Being a mathematics major, though, had rather little to do with knowing what to do with data. So I went on to a PhD in statistics, and picked up the biology I needed along the way.

Teaching and advising

I advise students in both Statistics and Data Science and the Graduate Group in Genomics and Computational Biology, and the lab has always been a mix of the two.
